# Welcome to U DU DU - Authentic Vietnamese Cuisine

## 1. About U DU DU
U DU DU is a family-owned Vietnamese restaurant located at **122 Hoang Quoc Viet, Cau Giay, Hanoi**. The restaurant was founded in **2015** with a mission to bring authentic Vietnamese flavors to both locals and tourists.

The name “U DU DU” is inspired by the Vietnamese word for **papaya**, a fruit symbolizing freshness and health. The restaurant prides itself on using **fresh, locally sourced ingredients** and traditional cooking techniques.

## 2. Cuisine and Signature Dishes
At U DU DU, we serve a variety of traditional **Vietnamese dishes**, including:

### **Main Courses**
- **Phở Bò Đặc Biệt (Special Beef Pho)** – A flavorful broth slow-cooked for **12 hours**, served with rice noodles, thinly sliced beef, and fresh herbs.
- **Bún Chả Hà Nội (Hanoi Grilled Pork with Vermicelli)** – A Hanoi specialty featuring grilled pork patties and sliced pork belly, served with rice vermicelli, fresh herbs, and dipping sauce.
- **Chả Cá Lã Vọng (Turmeric Fish with Dill)** – A famous dish from Hanoi, featuring grilled fish marinated in turmeric, served with vermicelli, peanuts, and fresh herbs.
- **Gỏi Cuốn (Fresh Spring Rolls)** – Rice paper wrapped with shrimp, vermicelli, fresh herbs, and served with peanut dipping sauce.
- **Cơm Chiên Hải Sản (Seafood Fried Rice)** – Stir-fried jasmine rice with shrimp, squid, and fresh vegetables.

### **Desserts & Drinks**
- **Bánh Flan (Caramel Flan)** – A smooth, creamy custard dessert topped with caramelized sugar.
- **Chè Khúc Bạch (Lychee & Almond Sweet Soup)** – A refreshing dessert made with almond jelly, lychee, and coconut milk.
- **Cà Phê Trứng (Egg Coffee)** – A Hanoi specialty made with Vietnamese robusta coffee topped with a creamy egg yolk foam.
- **Sinh Tố (Fruit Smoothies)** – Freshly blended drinks with mango, avocado, or passion fruit.
